# Hi, I'm Janindu Bandara

Master's student in Electrical Communication Engineering at the University of Kassel, currently completing my thesis at **Rohde & Schwarz GmbH & Co. KG** on pre-6G waveform optimization.

My work focuses on implementing and benchmarking next-generation waveform candidates (FDSS with spectral extension, OTFS modulation) against the 5G NR uplink baseline, involving constrained optimization, MATLAB TX/RX chain development, and performance evaluation under realistic channel conditions.
